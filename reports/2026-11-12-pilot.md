# Pilot report, 2026-11-12

> **Note (2026-11-16):** some numbers and verdicts in this report were superseded (coordinate fix, loss method,
> event selection, Hellisheiði definitions, citations). See [docs/corrections.md](../docs/corrections.md); the
> current values are in the README.

Scope: three pilots on free data, run end to end in one day. The numbers are in the README tables and the CSVs in
`docs/data/`. This report covers what worked, what did not, and what was not checked.

## What worked

- **Open data paths verified:**
  - NGL IGS20 `.tenv3` for 7 Reykjanes/Hengill stations, through 17 Oct 2026.
  - The **IMO Quakes API** (`https://api.vedur.is/quakes/`). It is documented by an OpenAPI spec, licensed
    CC BY 4.0, and needs no key. This is the "Quakes API" announced in Feb 2026.
  - LiCSAR frames 016A_02504_162118 and 155D_02484_191814, found by reading the bounds of all 710 frames on
    LiCSAR's volcano frame status page.
- **Pilot A (positive control) passes:**
  - Source depth 3.25-4.75 km (published 4-5 km).
  - Inflow rates match Parks et al. 2025 (7-9 m³/s at first, 2.4-4 m³/s in 2024).
  - Recharge before each of the last five events is 17.5-21.2 Mm³ (published 17-23).
  - Current-cycle volume 28.0/29.2 Mm³ vs IMO's 28.3/29.1 Mm³ on the same dates.
- **Volume-threshold check:** the current cycle (31.2 Mm³ by 17 Oct 2026, 457 days) is outside the range of every
  earlier pre-dike recharge since March 2024, and its inflow is the lowest yet (0.44 m³/s over Jul-Oct 2026).
- **Pilot B:**
  - The Nov 2023 dike is recovered: N37°E, 15.3 km long, 5 km median depth.
  - The current cycle is the seismically quietest inter-event period by a factor of 3.4.
  - SIL and SeisComP magnitudes differ by 0.62 units.
- **Pilot C:**
  - Reykjanes and Svartsengi pre-unrest subsidence agree with published rates.
  - The plate-spreading gradient shows up with opposite sign in the ascending and descending maps (sanity check).

## What failed or is weak

- **Single-station volume losses:** wrong for dikes that pass near SENG (events 1, 2, 3, 5, 10). The 10 Nov 2023
  loss comes out as 20 Mm³ against the published 77.8.
- **Mogi fit for Aug 2025-Oct 2026:** RMS 49 mm. One point source does not explain SENG and the Grindavík stations
  together. Not resolved.
- **Hellisheiði peak:** −14.6 mm/yr LOS (median 2015-2019) against the published 24-26 mm/yr (2012-2015,
  TerraSAR-X). Not resolved whether the rate dropped or the LiCSAR resolution smooths the peak.
- **Missing LiCSAR pairs:** 6 ascending and 9 descending pairs (2019-2022 primaries) listed by LiCSAR return HTTP
  403/404. There is no descending data after 2019 in this set.
- **SENG equipment offsets:** NGL's SENG series has two undocumented ±0.5 m vertical offsets (2024-09-05,
  2025-08-19), corrected here. If they are not artefacts, the volume series would be wrong by ~35 Mm³ between those
  dates. The evidence that they are artefacts: no horizontal motion, no event, they nearly cancel, and the
  corrected series matches IMO.

## Not verified

- EGMS: not downloaded (needs an account).
- ICEL-NMAR catalogue: not used.
- IMO's own GNSS stations (THOB, SKSH, ELDC): not in NGL. IMO GNSS data access was not explored.
- Seismicity relocation: not attempted. Magnitudes are local (`ML_SIL`), not Mw.
- Svartsengi production data (HS Orka): not found openly, so geothermal vs magmatic separation is only a bound.

## Data volume

About 150 MB in total (data/, git-ignored): LiCSAR crops 71 MB, IMO catalogues 21 MB, NGL 16 MB, two papers 40 MB.
